A man in India beat a mountain with a hammer.

In nineteen sixty, a poor labourer in Bihar named Dashrath Manjhi picked a fight.

Every single day, he climbed the hill and hit it.

The village laughed. They called him a madman.

One year became five. Five became twenty.

The mountain didn't fight back. It was too busy losing.

You see, it started when his wife fell on that hill.

The nearest doctor was fifty-five kilometres around it. She didn't make it.

After twenty-two years, he broke through. Fifty-five kilometres became fifteen.

He couldn't get her past the mountain. So he moved the mountain.
