DESCRIPTION
Ever wondered what a cup of coffee really does inside you? Within minutes caffeine slips from your gut into your blood, races to your brain and takes adenosine's "parking spots", so your brain never gets the message that it's tired. Your body releases adrenaline, your heart speeds up, the blood vessels in your head tighten and your gut wakes up. With a half-life of about five hours, an afternoon coffee is still half in your blood at bedtime, and when it finally wears off, all that built-up adenosine hits at once. That's the crash.

This video is for general education, not medical advice. Most healthy adults can have up to about 400 mg of caffeine a day (roughly 4 cups of brewed coffee); talk to a doctor if you're pregnant, have a heart condition or are sensitive to caffeine.
