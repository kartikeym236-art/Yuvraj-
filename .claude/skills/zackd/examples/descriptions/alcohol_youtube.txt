DESCRIPTION
If you drink a beer, the alcohol soaks into your blood and reaches your brain within minutes. It boosts the signals that slow your brain down and blocks the ones that speed it up. It also switches off the hormone that tells your kidneys to save water, and your liver can only clear about one drink an hour, turning it first into a toxic chemical called acetaldehyde.

This video is for education only and is not medical advice.
