TITLE
What Happens If You Don't Drink Water All Day

ALTERNATIVE TITLES
- What Skipping Water Does To Your Body
- Why Not Drinking Water Gives You A Headache
- Your Body After A Day Without Water

DESCRIPTION
If you go all day without drinking water, your blood gets thicker and saltier. A tiny sensor in your brain notices and tells your kidneys to hold on to water, so your pee turns dark. Your heart beats faster because there's less blood to go around, and your brain can shrink slightly and pull away from the skull. That's one cause of a dehydration headache. A glass of water starts reaching your blood within about five minutes.

This video is for general education only, not medical advice. If you have severe or ongoing symptoms, talk to a doctor.

Sources:
