The Most Important Company In The AI Race (It's Not NVIDIA)

DESCRIPTION
Every iPhone, every AI chip and every NVIDIA GPU depends on one company: ASML, from Veldhoven in the Netherlands. It is the only company that builds EUV lithography machines, which are needed to make the world's most advanced chips. One machine is about the size of a school bus, costs around $200 million, has over 100,000 parts and ships in 40 containers and several cargo planes. Inside, a laser hits tiny droplets of molten tin 50,000 times a second, making a light hotter than the surface of the sun that prints circuits thousands of times thinner than a human hair onto silicon. That's why the US pushed the Netherlands to block sales of these machines to China.
