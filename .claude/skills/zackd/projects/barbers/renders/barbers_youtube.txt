TITLE
Which Barber Would You Choose?

ALTERNATIVE TITLES
- The Two Barbers Puzzle (Most People Get It Wrong)
- Clean Shop Or Messy Shop? The Smart Answer
- Why You Should Pick The Barber With The Worst Haircut

DESCRIPTION
You're new in town and there are only two barbers. One shop is spotless and the barber has a perfect haircut. The other is a mess and the barber's hair looks terrible. Which one do you pick? Barbers can't cut their own hair - so each one's haircut was done by the other. The messy barber is the skilled one. Don't judge someone by their appearance; judge them by their work.
