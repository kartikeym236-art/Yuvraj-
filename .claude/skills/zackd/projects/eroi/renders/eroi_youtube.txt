TITLE
We Will Stop Using Oil - But Not Because It Runs Out

ALTERNATIVE TITLES
- EROI: The Real Reason Oil Will End
- Why We'll Stop Pumping Oil While It's Still In The Ground
- Half The Oil, Just To Get The Oil

DESCRIPTION
Pro-oil people say we'll never run out of oil - and they're right. We'll stop for a different reason: EROI, Energy Return on Investment. Getting oil out of the ground takes energy - drills, pumps, trucks, ships. Around 1930, America spent about one barrel's worth of energy to get a hundred barrels out. Today, a study in the journal Applied Energy estimates it takes about fifteen, and by 2050 it projects fifty - half the oil, just to get the oil. When it takes one barrel to get one barrel back, pumping stops, even with oil still underground. Fusion has been "a few decades away" for seventy years, and solar still depends on diesel, ships and trucks. The answer: see the abundance we still have, slow down, get some solar panels, learn real skills and get involved in your community.
