TITLE
What A Painkiller Actually Does Inside You

ALTERNATIVE TITLES
How Ibuprofen Really Stops Pain
Your Painkiller Doesn't Know Where Your Pain Is
What Happens After You Swallow Ibuprofen

DESCRIPTION
If you take ibuprofen for a headache, it doesn't fly straight to your head. It dissolves in your stomach, and your blood carries it all over your body. Where you're hurting, your cells make chemicals called prostaglandins, which make your pain nerves more sensitive. Ibuprofen blocks the enzyme that makes them (cyclooxygenase, or COX), so in about 20 to 30 minutes the pain fades. But prostaglandins also help protect your stomach lining, which is why it's best to take ibuprofen with food or milk. And the pill never even knew where your pain was.

Always follow the dose on the pack or your pharmacist's advice. Some people should not take ibuprofen (for example with certain stomach, kidney or heart problems, or in late pregnancy).
