DESCRIPTION
Elon Musk keeps raising his promise: first one SpaceX share for every child in America, then two, and now two shares every single year. Do the math: about 70 million kids x 2 shares x ~$130 a share is roughly $18 billion a year, and a child born today could own dozens of shares by adulthood. Why shares and not cash? A share makes kids curious - about SpaceX, rockets and Mars - and that curiosity can create the next generation of engineers. And if Tesla and SpaceX merge, as some reports suggest, one share could include rockets, AI, robots and self-driving cars.

Is it genius, a publicity stunt, or a promise that will never happen? Tell me in the comments.
