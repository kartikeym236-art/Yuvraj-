DESCRIPTION
It all started with oil. In the early 1950s a British company controlled Iran's oil and Iran got only about 16 percent of the profits. When Prime Minister Mohammad Mosaddegh nationalized it in 1951, the CIA and British intelligence overthrew him in 1953 (Operation Ajax) and restored the Shah, who ruled for 26 years while American companies got a 40 percent share of Iran's oil. In 1979 Iranians rose up, Ayatollah Khomeini took power, and students held 52 Americans hostage for 444 days. Then came the Iran-Iraq war, sanctions, the 2015 nuclear deal, its end in 2018, and direct strikes in 2025. Seventy years later, it's still not over.
