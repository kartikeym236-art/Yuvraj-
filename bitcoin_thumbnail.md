# Bitcoin, Explained: Thumbnail Prompt

Same layout as the reference thumbnail: a hero portrait on the left, a busy bird's-eye illustrated city behind, a giant title with years at top right, a logo tile, and a column of glowing icons down the right edge. The person is swapped for bitcoin's anonymous creator. His real face has never been seen, so he is drawn as a hooded figure whose face is hidden in shadow. That suits the mystery in the video, and the prompt names no real person, so image tools shouldn't block it.

## Main prompt

```text
FORMAT: YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 widescreen (1280x720), full-bleed, no borders.

STYLE: highly detailed hand-inked comic / graphic-novel illustration with bold black ink outlines, fine cross-hatching and texture lines, rich saturated colours, crisp cel shading and soft glowing highlights; a bright, busy, eye-catching YouTube documentary thumbnail look.

LEFT THIRD (hero figure, large, from mid-thigh up, filling the full height of the left side): the anonymous creator of bitcoin, a calm, confident man standing with his arms crossed, wearing a dark charcoal-black hoodie with the hood pulled up over his head. His face is completely hidden in deep shadow inside the hood; only a faint hint of a confident smirk is visible, with a subtle bitcoin-orange glow reflecting on his chin. Realistic adult proportions, detailed fabric folds and stitching on the hoodie. Around his whole silhouette, a thin glowing orange-and-white sticker-style cut-out outline separates him from the background.

BACKGROUND (behind and to the right of him, seen from a high bird's-eye isometric angle): a sprawling, detailed illustrated town under a bright blue sky with fluffy white clouds and green rolling hills on the horizon. In the middle, a huge roofless warehouse cut away to show long rows of black bitcoin mining machines connected by glowing bitcoin-orange cables. Around it: a classic columned bank building with a crack running down its front, a tall glass office tower, delivery trucks carrying crates, and in the lower middle a small retro corner pizzeria with a bright red-and-white awning and a big sign reading "PIZZA". Crowds of tiny people fill the streets and sidewalks, many holding phones, pointing and chatting.

TOP RIGHT TEXT: huge bold black serif headline "BITCOIN" across the top right, and directly below it, slightly smaller in the same bold black serif font, "2008 – 2026". Both have a subtle white outer glow so they pop off the sky. Spell the text exactly.

LOGO TILE: just to the right of the headline, a glossy rounded-square app-icon tile in bitcoin orange (#F7931A) with a bold white bitcoin "₿" symbol, with a soft glow and a slight 3D bevel.

RIGHT EDGE (vertical column of floating glowing icons, overlapping the city, each with a soft neon glow and sparkles): from top to bottom, a shiny gold bitcoin coin with the "₿" symbol; a chain of glowing translucent blue cubes (the blockchain); an industrial bitcoin mining rig box with fans; a golden key with a "₿" on its head; a pizza slice with a stretchy cheese pull; a padlock engraved "21M"; and at the bottom right a big bright orange zig-zag arrow shooting upward like a rising price chart.

COLOUR & LIGHT: bitcoin orange and gold as the hero accent glow (replacing the green of the reference), sky blue, grass green, warm brick reds, steel greys; bright daylight with glowing neon accents; high contrast and very clickable.

AVOID: any visible face inside the hood, any real person's likeness, real company logos, extra text, misspelled text, watermarks, blurry details, photorealism, 3D render look, dark gloomy overall image.

ASPECT RATIO: 16:9 --ar 16:9
```

## Alternative hero figure (if you'd rather show a face)

Swap the LEFT THIRD paragraph for this. It gives a recognisable "character" while still showing no real person:

```text
LEFT THIRD (hero figure, large, from mid-thigh up): an anonymous man with his arms crossed, wearing a dark charcoal hoodie with the hood up and a smooth plain white theatrical mask covering his whole face, the mask's eyes glowing faintly bitcoin orange; a confident, mysterious pose; thin glowing orange-and-white sticker-style cut-out outline around his silhouette.
```

## Tips
- Make 3–4 versions and pick the one where the hood shadow reads clearly at small size; thumbnails are mostly seen tiny on phones.
- If the tool garbles the title text, generate the image without the text (remove the TOP RIGHT TEXT section) and add "BITCOIN" and "2008 – 2026" yourself in an editor, using a bold serif font such as Playfair Display Black or DM Serif Display.
- "2008" is the year of the white paper; change it to "2009" if you'd rather mark the network's launch.
