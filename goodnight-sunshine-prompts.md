# 🌙 Goodnight, My Sunshine — Image & Animation Prompts

> Every prompt below is **self-sufficient**: it repeats the full character codes, location, props, style and negatives, so you can paste any single prompt into an image or video generator on its own.
>
> **Copy:** hover over any grey prompt box on GitHub and click the 📋 copy icon in its top-right corner (one click copies the whole prompt).

## Workflow

1. Generate the **Image Prompt** for a scene (keep the same seed / reference image for character consistency).
2. Upload that image to your image-to-video tool (Kling, Runway, Veo, Hailuo, Higgsfield, etc.).
3. Paste the matching **Animation Prompt**.
4. Tip: after Scene 01, use the boy's face from Scene 01 and the father's face from Scene 08 as character reference images for later scenes.

## Scene 01

**Script line:** *Every night, a little boy received a message from his late mother's phone.*

### 🖼️ Image Prompt — Scene 01

```text
SCENE 01 – ESTABLISHING: the nightly message.
[LOC_BEDROOM_01] a small cozy child's bedroom at night: a single wooden bed against the back wall with a navy-blue quilt printed with white clouds, a worn brown teddy bear with one button eye on the pillow, glow-in-the-dark star stickers on a sloped ceiling, a small wooden nightstand to the right of the bed holding a yellow crescent-moon night lamp and a silver-framed photo of the mother, a window on the left wall with sheer white curtains letting in cool blue moonlight, and a white bedroom door on the right wall that opens onto a dim hallway lit by a warm amber light

CHARACTERS:
[CHAR_BOY_01] a 7-year-old South Asian boy, small and slim, warm medium-brown skin, soft round face, big round dark-brown eyes with long lashes, short slightly messy jet-black hair with a cowlick sticking up at the crown, a small gap between his two front teeth, wearing faded sky-blue long-sleeve cotton pajamas with a tiny yellow star print, barefoot, a thin red thread bracelet on his right wrist

PROPS: [PROP_KIDPHONE_01] the boy's small older smartphone in a chunky bright-orange rubber kid-proof case with two little side handles. [CHAR_MOM_01] the late mother, seen ONLY inside a silver-framed photograph: a 34-year-old South Asian woman with long wavy black hair over one shoulder, a warm bright smile, a small gold nose stud, wearing a mustard-yellow cotton kurta, laughing in golden sunlight

SHOT: wide shot, slightly high angle, camera near the window looking toward the bed. The boy lies under the navy cloud quilt hugging the one-eyed teddy bear, eyes open, unable to sleep. On the nightstand his orange-case phone has just lit up, its screen glowing and casting a soft white-blue light across his cheek. Right next to the phone stands the silver-framed photo of his smiling mother, half lit by the screen. Moonlight streams through the sheer curtains, the ceiling stars glow faintly green. Quiet, lonely, tender atmosphere.

STYLE: cinematic 3D animated feature-film style, soft subsurface skin shading, expressive large eyes, realistic cloth and hair detail, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh, gentle film grain, emotional and heartwarming mood, two-tone lighting palette of cool moonlight blue (#3A5A8C) and warm amber lamp glow (#FFB347), vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, 4K, ultra-detailed, masterpiece.
NEGATIVE: text errors, misspelled words, extra fingers, deformed hands, distorted face, extra limbs, horror, ghost, scary, creepy, blood, mother appearing in person, inconsistent clothing, different hairstyle, watermark, logo, signature, blurry, low quality, oversaturated.
```
