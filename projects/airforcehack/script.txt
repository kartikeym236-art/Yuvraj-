The most powerful air force on Earth just got hacked. And the attackers didn't need a single missile.
According to Reuters, hackers broke into a cloud platform the {US|u s} Air Force uses to manage military operations. Not a public website. The system that helps plan and coordinate missions.
Here's why that's terrifying. Modern militaries don't run on paper anymore. Flight schedules, supply routes, maintenance records, even where aircraft are positioned. A lot of it lives in the cloud. So if someone gets inside, they don't need to shoot down a jet. They can learn where it's going, slow it down, or quietly watch everything.
And this isn't the first warning. Just days ago, Homeland Security said America's critical infrastructure was under attack. Government agencies have been breached. And Britain openly admits it's running cyber operations against Russia, China, Iran and North Korea.
Officials haven't said who did it or what was taken. But one thing is clear. The next war might not start with explosions. It might start with a login screen.
