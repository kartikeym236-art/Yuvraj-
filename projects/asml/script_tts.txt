Every iPhone, every AI chip, every Nvidia GPU depends on one company you've probably never heard of. And it's not in America.
It's ASML, a Dutch company in a small town called Veldhoven. And it's the only company on Earth that can build the machine needed to make the world's most advanced chips.
The machine is called an EUV lithography machine. It's the size of a school bus, costs around 200 million dollars, and has over a hundred thousand parts. Shipping one takes forty containers and multiple cargo planes.
Here's how it works. It fires a laser at tiny droplets of molten tin, fifty thousand times every second, creating a light hotter than the surface of the sun. That light carves circuits thousands of times thinner than a human hair onto silicon.
TSMC, Samsung and Intel all depend on it. Without ASML, there's no cutting-edge AI.
That's why it's now a geopolitical weapon. The US pushed the Netherlands to block sales of these machines to China. And China, despite spending billions, still can't build one.
So the most important company in the AI race isn't Nvidia, Apple or OpenAI. It's a Dutch company that makes machines that make chips.
