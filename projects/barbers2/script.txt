You're new in town and you need a haircut. There are only two barbers. Which one do you choose?
The first shop is spotless. The floor is swept, the mirrors shine, and the barber himself has a perfect, sharp haircut.
The second shop is a mess. Hair everywhere, a cracked mirror, and the barber's own hair looks terrible.
Most people pick the first barber. But that's the wrong choice.
Here's why. There are only two barbers in town. Barbers can't cut their own hair. So who gave the first barber his perfect haircut? The second barber. And who gave the second barber that terrible haircut? The first one.
So the messy barber is actually the skilled one. His shop is messy because he's busy. Everyone smart goes to him.
The lesson? Don't judge someone by their appearance. Judge them by their work.
