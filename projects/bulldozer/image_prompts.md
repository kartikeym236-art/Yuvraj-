# The Man Who Enjoys His Pain: 2x2 grid prompts (5 images = 20 scenes)

Each prompt makes ONE square 1:1 image split into a 2x2 grid of four square panels. Save the images as `01.png`, `02.png`, ... in order.

## 01  ·  scenes 1-4

- top-left: "Nothing is more dangerous"
- top-right: "than a man who has started to enjoy his pain."
- bottom-left: "There's a rare type of person"
- bottom-right: "that has played the game long enough"

```
FORMAT: ONE single square 1:1 image divided into a 2x2 GRID of FOUR equal square panels, separated only by thin straight uniform white gutter lines; no outer border, no panel numbers. Each panel is a separate full-bleed illustration with its own composition. All four panels share exactly the same art style, character design, colours and lighting.

STYLE: Soft hand-drawn pencil-and-watercolour sketch illustration, like a gentle storybook. Thin, slightly shaky graphite-grey ink lines with light pencil hatching for shadows; very pale, washed-out watercolour tints. CHARACTER: a simple stick figure with a large perfectly round head coloured pale cream-ivory (soft warm off-white, slightly yellowish), a thin dark-grey outline, two or three short wispy hair strands on top, two small black dot eyes, a tiny curved line for a mouth, sometimes small eyebrows for emotion, no nose; thin single-line stick arms and legs, small rounded hands; wears a simple pale grey-white draped cloth tunic. The character is medium-small in the frame with lots of empty space. BACKGROUND: airy, minimal, faded landscape of pale sand-beige desert plains, soft rolling dunes and grey-blue distant hills, scattered small pebbles, tiny tufts of grass and a few sketchy olive trees, a near-white cream sky with a few faint pencil cloud lines. Palette is very light and desaturated: cream, ivory, sand, pale grey-blue and dusty sage; lots of white paper showing through; quiet, calm mood. NO text, letters or numbers anywhere.

PANEL 1 (TOP-LEFT): The stick man standing at the bottom of a steep rocky hill, looking up at it, calm and determined.
PANEL 2 (TOP-RIGHT): The stick man with a scraped knee, small bandage on his arm, smiling softly while sitting on a rock.
PANEL 3 (BOTTOM-LEFT): One stick man standing apart from a small group of other stick figures on a dusty plain.
PANEL 4 (BOTTOM-RIGHT): The stick man walking along a long winding path across the desert, many footprints behind him.
```
