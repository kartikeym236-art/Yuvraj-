# Is Your Blue the Same as Mine?: 2x2 grid prompts (8 images = 32 scenes)

Each prompt makes ONE square 1:1 image split into a 2x2 grid of four square panels. Save the images as `01.png`, `02.png`, ... in order.

## 01  ·  scenes 1-4

- top-left: "The sky is blue."
- top-right: "Grass is green."
- bottom-left: "Fire is orange."
- bottom-right: "Everyone agrees, right?"

```
FORMAT: ONE single square 1:1 image divided into a 2x2 GRID of FOUR equal square panels, separated only by thin straight uniform white gutter lines; no outer border, no panel numbers. Each panel is a separate full-bleed illustration with its own composition. All four panels share exactly the same art style, character design, colours and lighting.

STYLE: Hand-drawn illustration. Simple stick-figure character: a large perfectly round head in soft beige-peach with a thin black ink outline, light pencil shading on one side, a small curl of a few hair strands on top, tiny black dot eyes, a small faint mouth, a soft blush on the cheek, and a body, arms and legs drawn as single thin black ink lines. Backgrounds painted in loose muted watercolour washes: misty rolling sage-green hills, a calm slate-grey lake, a soft sepia-beige sky, sparse trees and grass strokes, gentle paper texture. Calm, minimal compositions with lots of empty space; desaturated palette of sepia, sand, sage and slate, with soft colour accents only where the scene is about a colour. NO text, letters or numbers anywhere.

PANEL 1 (TOP-LEFT): The stick man on a hill looking up at a soft blue watercolour sky.
PANEL 2 (TOP-RIGHT): The stick man touching tall green grass in a meadow.
PANEL 3 (BOTTOM-LEFT): The stick man warming his hands at a small orange campfire by the lake.
PANEL 4 (BOTTOM-RIGHT): A few stick figures standing together nodding.
```
