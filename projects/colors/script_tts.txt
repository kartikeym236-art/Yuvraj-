The sky is blue. Grass is green. Fire is orange. Everyone agrees, right? But here's something no one can prove. What if the blue you see is not the blue I see?
This is the colorblind paradox. Since we were kids, someone pointed at the sky and said, "That's blue." So we all learned the same word. But what that color actually looks like inside your head, nobody else can ever see it. Your blue could be my green. Your red could be my purple. We'd still agree on everything and never find out.
Here's the twist. It's not just colors. Maybe nobody experiences the world exactly the way you do. Your sunset, your favorite song, the way rain feels, all yours, and no one else will ever see it quite the same.
