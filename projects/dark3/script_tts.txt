Season 2 ends with Adam killing Martha. Then another Martha appears and pulls Jonas into her world. It's a mirror version of Winden where Jonas was never born, and her world is heading toward its own apocalypse.
Jonas learns there are two masters. Adam, his future self, wants to destroy time. Eva, Martha's future self, wants to protect the loop at any cost. Jonas and Martha are just their pieces on the board.
Both worlds end on the same day. Jonas and Martha keep jumping through time, from 1888 to 2053, and every move they make only ties the knot tighter.
In 1954, Jonas's mother Hannah has a daughter, Silja. Silja becomes the ancestor of Noah and Agnes. Everyone in Winden turns out to be part of the loop.
Martha is pregnant with Jonas's child. That child grows up to be "The Unknown," who lives in both worlds and keeps the loop going.
Adam goes back to 1888 and kills the pregnant Martha to break the cycle. It changes nothing. The knot survives.
Claudia shows Adam what he's missed. There's a third world, the original one. In it, the clockmaker Tannhaus loses his son and granddaughter in a car crash. Out of grief, he builds a time machine, and that machine split reality into the two broken worlds.
At the moment of the apocalypse, Jonas and Martha meet between the worlds. They travel to the origin world on the night of the crash and stop the car. Tannhaus never builds the machine, so both worlds disappear, along with Jonas, Martha, Adam and Eva.
In the origin world, Hannah sits at a dinner party and says she always liked the name Jonas. The lights flicker.
The beginning was the end, and the end was the beginning. Follow for more.
