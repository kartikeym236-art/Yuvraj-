Pro-oil people say we'll never run out of oil. And they're right. Just not the way they think.
We'll be forced to stop using oil because of one term: EROI. Energy Return on Investment.
Pumping oil burns energy. Drills, pumps, trucks, ships. In 1930, America used about one barrel's worth of energy to get a hundred barrels out. Today, one study in the journal Applied Energy estimates it takes about fifteen. By 2050, it projects fifty. Half the oil, just to get the oil.
And you don't need to read the studies. Just look around. In the early 1900s, almost all our oil came from easy wells on land. Now we build massive rigs out at sea and crack rock apart to get it. Nobody does that for oil that's easy to reach.
So what happens when it takes one barrel of oil to get one barrel back? We stop. The oil's still down there. There's just no reason to pump it.
The big hope is fusion. But fusion has been "a few decades away" for over seventy years. And green tech can't build more green tech, not yet. Solar panels still need diesel to dig up the metal, ships burning oil to carry it, and trucks to bring it to you.
So the key is to see how much abundance we still have. Slow down. Get some solar panels. Learn real skills. Get involved in your community. If you're prepared, the decline can lead to a better way of life.
