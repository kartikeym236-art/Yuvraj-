Three signs of a fake friend.
Number one. They only call you for help. The moment that you ask for help. they don't give it. But the moment they ask. they expect it right away. They only want you for help.
Number two. They gossip about your privacy. Every time you tell them something in private. and you tell them. "Hey. please don't share this with anybody." and you open up to them. they gossip that to all the other friends. They open up to everybody else. they share your problems. That's not a friend.
Number three. They keep making jokes that hurt you. Even though you tell them. "Hey bro. you know what? Don't say that. that hurts me. You know I'm insecure about that." or. "You know I went through a hard time in my life when that happened." and they keep making jokes about it. That's not a real friend. You've told them to stop. and they keep on proceeding with it.
