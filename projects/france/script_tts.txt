France's entire financial system is crashing before our very eyes, because basically everything that could go wrong in an economy is happening in France at the exact same time. The country's debt just hit a hard 120% of GDP, bond yields are hitting 20-year highs, inflation is over 3%, and worst of all, at the same time, unemployment just hit a high of 8.3% last month. And the thing is, France can't fix one problem without making the other ones significantly worse. The first problem is the debt, as France is running record deficits. Right now government spending accounts for 57% of the entire country's GDP. If they cut spending to fix the deficit, they also have to cut jobs, which will make unemployment go up even further. If they raise rates to counteract inflation, that means bond yields will also go up and the interest they have to pay on their record debt goes up as well. This would also contract the economy and still make unemployment worse. If France wanted to fix their 8.3% unemployment, they would have to reduce rates and increase government spending to stimulate the economy. This would cause government debt to spiral even further and inflation would go up from the current 3%. So it makes sense why there are mass riots in the country right now. Citizens are hurting and want major change, yet France's politicians are stuck because making a change could break the system even further.
