You only exist because of incest. A lot of incest. In order for you to have been born, you had to have had two parents, and each of those parents had to have had two parents, and so on. That means after every generation your number of ancestors doubles. After {10|ten} generations, you have {1,000|one thousand}, and after {20|twenty}, you have {1 million|one million}. But what if we go back to the Roman Republic around the year zero? Well, with just {67|sixty seven} generations of humans, if every ancestor were unique, you'd have over {100 quintillion|one hundred quintillion} direct ancestors. But since there have only been about {120 billion|one hundred twenty billion} humans to exist, by the pigeonhole principle, this is clearly impossible. This phenomenon is known as pedigree collapse. Instead of your family tree being a perfect binary tree, it actually collapses in on itself with the same people appearing over and over again. This means that mathematically distant or close relatives must have gone to town. But that shouldn't be too surprising. Throughout history, incest was very common, especially in royal families. For example, the Habsburg family tree looks less like a tree and more like a line. But even recently, Albert Einstein, Edgar Allan Poe, and Charles Darwin all married their first cousins.
