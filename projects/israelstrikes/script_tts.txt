How many countries has Israel actually struck? Not threatened. Struck. The real list is longer than most people think.
Start with the obvious ones. Gaza and the West Bank. Lebanon, where it has fought Hezbollah for decades. Syria, where it has hit targets hundreds of times. Iran, where it struck nuclear and military sites. Iraq, where in 1981 its jets destroyed a nuclear reactor. And Yemen, where it has bombed Houthi ports and power stations.
Then it gets surprising. Qatar, where in 2025 Israel struck Hamas leaders in Doha, inside a close American ally. Sudan, where it reportedly hit weapons convoys heading to Gaza. Tunisia, where in 1985 it bombed the headquarters of the Palestine Liberation Organization, more than a thousand miles from home.
Uganda, where in 1976 Israeli commandos flew thousands of miles to Entebbe airport and rescued over a hundred hostages in ninety minutes. And Egypt and Jordan, where it fought full-scale wars before signing peace deals.
And that's just the open strikes. Israeli intelligence has been linked to covert operations across Europe, Dubai and beyond.
That's more than a dozen countries across the Middle East and Africa. Supporters call it self-defense against enemies who want Israel gone. Critics call it a regional war that never stops expanding.
