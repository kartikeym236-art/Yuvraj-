Here are five signs that someone might be manipulating you:
{#1|number one} Guilt Tripping.
Every time you say no to something, they make you feel terrible about it. "I did so much for you, and you can't even do this much?" Gradually, you start prioritizing their feelings over your own happiness.
{#2|number two} Gaslighting.
Imagine you saw something happen with your own eyes, but they insist it never took place at all and that you are just overreacting. Over time, instead of doubting their words, you begin to doubt your own memory.
{#3|number three} Love Bombing.
In the beginning, they shower you with an overwhelming amount of love and attention, making you feel like you could never find anyone better. But as soon as you become emotionally attached, they start to change, leaving you constantly trying to bring back that earlier version of them.
{#4|number four} The Silent Treatment.
Instead of addressing what's wrong, they suddenly cut off all communication. No texts, no phone calls, and zero explanation. You end up being the one who constantly apologizes just to return things to normal.
{#5|number five} Moving the Goalposts.
You meet every single expectation they set, but as soon as you achieve it, a new demand appears. No matter how much effort you put in, it is never enough for them.
