# The Python Story: 2x2 grid prompts (9 images = 36 scenes)

Each prompt makes ONE square 1:1 image split into a 2x2 grid of four square panels. Save as `01.png`, `02.png`, ... in order.

## 01  ·  scenes 1-4

- top-left: "A woman let her pet python"
- top-right: "sleep beside her in bed every single night."
- bottom-left: "It was 10 ft long,"
- bottom-right: "and she loved it like a child."

```
FORMAT: ONE single square 1:1 image divided into a 2x2 GRID of FOUR equal square panels, separated only by thin straight uniform white gutter lines; no outer border, no panel numbers. Each panel is a separate full-bleed illustration with its own composition. All four panels share exactly the same art style, character designs, colours and lighting.

STYLE: Flat 2D cartoon illustration, full-bleed. Clean, smooth black ink outlines of even medium thickness, flat colours with soft simple shading. Characters are simple stick-figure people: a large perfectly round SOLID WHITE head with no skin colour, two small black oval dot eyes, short simple eyebrows for emotion, a tiny mouth line, NO nose; thin black stick arms and legs, small white mitten hands; simple flat clothes on a small torso. Backgrounds are detailed, clean flat-colour cartoon environments (bedroom, clinic, office, park) with simple perspective; night scenes use a dusky blue-purple palette with warm yellow lamp glow. NO text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.

CHARACTERS (identical in every panel):
- THE WOMAN: stick-figure woman with a solid white round head, long dark-brown hair, light pink t-shirt and grey pyjama pants.
- THE PYTHON: a very long (10 ft) cartoon python with cream and golden-brown patterned scales, simple friendly-looking eyes.

PANEL 1 (TOP-LEFT): THE WOMAN gently lifting THE PYTHON in a cosy apartment living room at dusk.
PANEL 2 (TOP-RIGHT): A dark bedroom, THE WOMAN asleep, THE PYTHON coiled on the pillow beside her, moonlight through blinds.
PANEL 3 (BOTTOM-LEFT): THE PYTHON stretched across the full length of the bedroom floor, the woman small beside it.
PANEL 4 (BOTTOM-RIGHT): THE WOMAN smiling, the python's head resting on her shoulder.
```
