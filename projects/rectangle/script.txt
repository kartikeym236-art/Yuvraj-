Humans are obsessed with rectangles. And everything we build and live in from birth until death is a rectangle. And turns out there's a pretty cool reason why. Yep, not only are rectangles just straight lines and right angles, which makes them easier to measure, cut, design, package, and build with, but it also means they fit flushly together with no wasted space and also creates a second flat surface to continue building on. So big rectangle kind of monopolized us.
