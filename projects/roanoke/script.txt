Chapter one. The race for a new world.

England's queen, Elizabeth the First, wants a piece of that. But England doesn't have a single permanent colony in the New World.

Enter Sir Walter Raleigh. In {1584|fifteen eighty four}, the queen gives him permission to claim land in North America and start a colony.

Raleigh sends a scouting trip first. They land on a small island called Roanoke, tucked between the mainland and the long, thin barrier islands of the Outer Banks. The scouts come home with glowing reports. Friendly locals. Rich soil. Plenty of food. A paradise.

They also bring two Native men back to England with them. Their names are Manteo and Wanchese. Remember those two names. They're going to matter a lot.

Chapter two. The colony before the Lost Colony.

Chapter seven. The dark theories.

Theory number one. The Spanish got them.

Spain knew the English were trying to settle in North America, and they hated the idea. Spanish ships did search the coast looking for the English settlement. But here's the problem.
