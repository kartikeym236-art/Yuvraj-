The person who created Bitcoin. Where is he today? Nobody knows. And here's the craziest part. Around 1.1 million Bitcoins are still sitting untouched in his wallets, worth roughly 100 billion dollars today.
This story begins in 2008. The world was in a financial crisis. Banks were failing, and people were losing trust in the banking system. Then, out of nowhere, someone calling himself Satoshi Nakamoto posts a nine-page paper online. It explained Bitcoin, a digital currency that needed no bank and no middleman.
In 2009, the very first block of Bitcoin was created, known as the Genesis Block. Inside it, Satoshi hid a newspaper headline about the government bailing out the banks. It was a direct jab at the traditional banking system.
In the early days, Satoshi mined Bitcoin himself and slowly built up around 1.1 million coins. Back then, they were worth almost nothing. Today, they're worth tens of billions of dollars.
Then, in 2011, Satoshi sent one last known message, saying he had moved on to other things. And then he vanished from the internet completely. Many people have claimed to be Satoshi since, but nobody has ever proved it.
The wildest part? The person who built Bitcoin disappeared, yet the network keeps running perfectly to this day.
