# You Are the Only Real Human: 2x2 grid prompts (6 images = 24 scenes)

Each prompt makes ONE square 1:1 image split into a 2x2 grid of four square panels. Save the images as `01.png`, `02.png`, ... in order.

## 01  ·  scenes 1-4

- top-left: "You are the only real human being living on this Earth."
- top-right: "Everyone around you is just your imagination,"
- bottom-left: "even me, whom you are watching in this video."
- bottom-right: "In this entire universe,"

```
FORMAT: ONE single square 1:1 image divided into a 2x2 GRID of FOUR equal square panels, separated only by thin straight uniform white gutter lines; no outer border, no panel numbers. Each panel is a separate full-bleed illustration with its own composition. All four panels share exactly the same art style, character design, colours and lighting.

STYLE: Hand-drawn storybook illustration with loose ink lines and soft painterly colour, exactly like a muted watercolour-and-gouache sketch. CHARACTER: a simple stick figure with a large perfectly round head coloured warm tan-beige (like a peach-brown ball) with a darker brown shading crescent on the lower side and a soft highlight on top, a thin black ink outline, three or four short thin black hair strands sticking up from the top, two tiny black dot eyes close together and slightly to one side, NO mouth, NO nose; a body, arms and legs drawn as single thin black ink lines, tiny rounded hands. The character is small in the frame with lots of space around him. BACKGROUND: muted painted landscape of rolling olive-green grassy hills with sketchy grass strokes, a calm slate-grey lake with soft ripples, distant brown-grey hills, and a warm hazy sepia-beige sky with soft horizontal brush strokes. Wooden props (signs, posts) are drawn in warm brown with visible wood grain. Desaturated earthy palette of sand, sepia, olive and slate; quiet, contemplative mood. NO text, letters or numbers anywhere.

PANEL 1 (TOP-LEFT): The stick man standing alone on a grassy hill by the lake, nobody else anywhere.
PANEL 2 (TOP-RIGHT): The stick man surrounded by faint ghostly outlines of other stick figures fading into the haze.
PANEL 3 (BOTTOM-LEFT): The stick man holding a small phone; on its screen a tiny stick figure waving back.
PANEL 4 (BOTTOM-RIGHT): The stick man tiny on the hill under a vast hazy sky with faint stars.
```
