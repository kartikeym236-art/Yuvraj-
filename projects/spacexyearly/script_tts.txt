Elon Musk just raised his promise again. He now says he'll give two SpaceX shares to every child in America, every single year.
First, he promised one share. People laughed and called it another random tweet. Then he doubled it to two. And now, it's two shares every year.
Do the math. America has about seventy million kids. At around a hundred and thirty dollars a share, that's roughly eighteen billion dollars in a single year. Do it every year, and a child born today could own dozens of SpaceX shares by adulthood.
So why shares and not cash? Because a share makes kids curious. They'll ask: What is SpaceX? How do rockets work? How do we get to Mars? That curiosity is how you create the next generation of engineers.
And if Tesla and SpaceX merge, as some reports suggest, each share could include rockets, AI, robots and self-driving cars.
