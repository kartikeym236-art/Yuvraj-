You should hate a character like Walter White, yet you don't. But why is that? At the start of the show, Walter's working two jobs, things are poor at home, he just got diagnosed with cancer, and he's pretty much going through life without purpose. You feel sympathy for him. And with each and every hardship continuously stacking up, Walter realizes he wants more out of life. He's tired of living without meaning, and deep down, you want the same thing. Walter is the underdog, and so are you. You want to see yourself win against all odds, and Walter is doing just that. You almost feel proud of him, as if he is a projection of you. And because you see yourself in him, it doesn't matter how bad he does, you find an excuse or a way around it to justify him.
