Why warm countries are poorer. And honestly, it completely changes how you look at the world map. If you look at the global wealth, almost all rich countries are in cold, temperate climates, and almost all poor countries are in the hot tropics.
First is the biological heat tax. Science shows that the optimal temperature for human economic productivity is actually around {13|thirteen} to {14°C|fourteen degrees celsius}, but in intense heat, your cardiovascular system diverts massive amounts of energy just to cool your internal organs. Cognitive function drops, physical labor becomes exhausting, and societal productivity naturally slows down.
Second, the agricultural trap. Tropical regions get heavy, constant rainfall, which creates a process called leaching, washing all the nutrients out of the soil. Meanwhile, in cold climates, winter freezes dead plants, trapping nutrients under the snow to decompose slowly. This creates deep, incredibly rich topsoil, perfect for mass agriculture.
Third, cold climates have a magical reset button: winter. Frost naturally kills pests, parasites, and diseases every single year. The tropics never get a break.
Finally, this all perfectly explains colonization. Europeans arrived in cold climates like the US or the Canada; they survived the familiar weather, so they settled down and built inclusive institutions: schools, hospitals, and property rights. But in the hot tropics, malaria and yellow fever killed them in droves. Because they couldn't settle safely, they built extractive institutions — brutal systems designed purely to extract resources, enslave locals, and ship wealth back to Europe.
