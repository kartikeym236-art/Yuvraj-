Loki wanted to get caught. And that one move almost destroyed Earth.

An alien called The Other, who works for someone way scarier, offers Loki a deal. He gets an army called the Chitauri, and in return he has to get them one thing: the Tesseract.

Loki shows up, steals the cube, and with a glowing scepter takes over Hawkeye's mind in two seconds. Nick Fury's base collapses behind him.

So Fury does the craziest thing possible. He brings together a billionaire with an ego problem, a ninety-year-old soldier, a man who turns into a monster when he gets angry, a spy, and a literal god.

What could go wrong?

Everything goes wrong. Loki surrenders on purpose. While he's locked up on their own ship, his scepter quietly turns the heroes against each other. Then Hawkeye attacks, Banner hulks out, and Loki kills Agent Coulson.

Fury uses that death to give them a reason to fight together. That's the moment they stop being strangers and become a team.

A portal rips open above Stark Tower and aliens flood New York. Cap calls the shots, Hawkeye covers the rooftops, and Hulk throws Loki around like a rag doll. Puny god.

Then the government fires a nuke at the city. Tony grabs it, flies it through the portal, and sees an alien fleet in deep space... right before his suit goes dark.

Hulk catches him and they win. But out in deep space, someone turns around... and smiles.

That's where Infinity War really began.
