A town has two barbers: one with a perfect haircut, and one with a terrible one. A stranger walks past the perfect one and sits down with the butcher. That's what the town calls him, because his hair looks like it was cut in the dark with a fork. So the whole street stops to watch. The barber with the perfect hair holds his door open and yells, "Last chance, friend!" Someone starts taking bets on whether the stranger keeps his ears. And soon, half the town is pressed against the window as the scissors go up. Then, the butcher spins the chair around, and it's the best haircut any of them have ever seen. Wait, what? You see, a barber can't cut his own hair, and this town only has two barbers, so they cut each other's. That perfect haircut across the street is the butcher's work, and the disaster on the butcher's head came from the man yelling "last chance." By morning, the line outside the butcher's shop wraps around the block, and the other barber has one way left to ruin him, which is to make him look good. So when the butcher comes in for his usual trim, he works on it for three hours, harder than he has ever worked in his life. The butcher walks out looking worse than ever, and the line gets longer.
