# Roanoke thumbnail: master prompt

Paste the main prompt into Gemini / ChatGPT image / Midjourney at 16:9.
Generate 4–6 versions, pick the one that reads best at phone size, then add the text yourself (see bottom).

## Main prompt (version A: "The word on the post")

```
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 (1280x720), bold and simple, readable at tiny phone size.

STYLE: flat 2D hand-drawn cartoon matching an educational history explainer: thick clean black outlines, flat colours, one hard cel-shade tone, simple "bean people" with big perfectly round beige heads, small black dot eyes, no nose, expressive eyebrows, thin stick arms and legs. Clean vector-like finish. NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D, NOT painterly, no glossy AI shine, no lens flare, no extra details.

COMPOSITION: one clear subject, nothing else competing.
- LEFT 45%: JOHN WHITE, an Elizabethan Englishman (grey hair, neat grey pointed beard, tall black brimmed hat, black doublet with small white ruff, dark-brown cloak), shown from the chest up and large in frame, turned three-quarters toward the right. His expression is real shock: eyebrows shot up, eyes wide, mouth a small open "o", one hand raised near his face. Readable emotion at a glance.
- RIGHT 55%: a tall, weathered wooden post in sharp focus, bark stripped off, with the single word CROATOAN carved deep into it in rough capital letters. The carving is the brightest, highest-contrast thing in the frame.
- BACKGROUND: an empty, abandoned settlement clearing at dusk: a dark sharpened-log palisade wall, faint dark pine forest, a stormy deep-teal sky. Background is simplified and slightly darker so the man and the post pop.

COLOUR & LIGHT: limited palette of three colours: deep teal/navy shadows, warm parchment-cream on the post and face, one hot accent of burnt orange from a low sunset glow rim-lighting the post and John White's face. Strong value contrast: bright subject, dark surroundings. Slight dark vignette at the corners.

DEPTH: the post and the man are in front and crisp; the background is flatter and lower contrast.

TEXT: no text except the carved word CROATOAN on the post, spelled exactly C-R-O-A-T-O-A-N, carved into the wood (not floating). Leave the top-left 30% of the frame calm and uncluttered for a title to be added later.

AVOID: extra or misspelled words, random letters, logos, watermarks, borders, cluttered crowds, multiple focal points, tiny details, realistic faces, noses, extra fingers, plastic skin, oversaturated neon colours, HDR look, motion blur, bokeh, gradients that look airbrushed, generic "AI art" glow.
```

## Alternative B: "They vanished" (eerie, high curiosity)

Same STYLE, COLOUR and AVOID blocks as A, with this COMPOSITION:

```
A small empty sandy clearing at dusk with abandoned cottages, seen from slightly above. In the centre, about 10 colonist bean-people (men, women, a child, Elizabethan clothes) are drawn as pale, semi-transparent ghostly outlines fading into thin air from bottom to top, like they are dissolving. One solid, fully coloured figure, JOHN WHITE, stands in the foreground left edge, small, back half-turned to camera, staring at them. A single wooden post on the right with CROATOAN carved on it. Cold teal light; the only warm colour is a faint orange sunset strip on the horizon.
```

## Alternative C: "Close-up hook" (simplest, strongest at small size)

```
Extreme close-up: a weathered wooden post fills the right two-thirds of the frame, the word CROATOAN carved deep in rough capitals, lit by warm orange low sunlight. A single human eye and eyebrow of JOHN WHITE (grey eyebrow, round beige cartoon face) peek in from the left edge, wide with shock. Dark stormy teal background. Everything else plain.
```

## Title text to add yourself (Canva / CapCut, not in the AI prompt)

AI image models misspell text, so add the title in an editor:
- 2–4 words maximum, very heavy font (Montserrat Black / Bebas / Anton), white with a thick black outline + soft shadow, one word in yellow #FFC816.
- Pick one:
  - **115 PEOPLE *VANISHED***
  - **WHERE DID THEY *GO?***
  - **ONE WORD *LEFT***
  - **THE *LOST* COLONY**
- Place it top-left, in the empty area you left. Never cover John White's face or the carved word.
- Optional: a small red arrow or red circle pointing at "CROATOAN" (one only).

## Final check (zoom to ~15% size, about phone-thumbnail size)
- Can you still read the emotion on his face and see the post? If not, enlarge the subject or simplify the background.
- No more than 3 main colours; one focal point; text ≤ 4 words.
- Different from the video title, so the thumbnail and title make one curiosity gap together. E.g. title "The Lost Colony of Roanoke Explained" + thumbnail "WHERE DID THEY GO?".
