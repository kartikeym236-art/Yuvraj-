# Scene Prompt Pack: Indian Slice-of-Life Cartoon Style

Copy-paste prompts for free image tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, Bing Image Creator).
Every prompt = **STYLE BLOCK + CHARACTER BLOCK(s) + SCENE BLOCK + NEGATIVE BLOCK**.
Paste all four parts every time, unchanged except the scene. That keeps the look identical across frames.

---

## 1. STYLE BLOCK (paste first, every time)

```
STYLE: Hand-drawn 2D Indian webcomic illustration, square 1:1 frame. Characters are drawn in a clean cartoon style: bold uniform black ink outlines (about 4px), large round head slightly wider than tall, simple flat peach-orange skin (#F2A36B) with a soft pink blush circle on the cheeks, tiny solid black oval dot eyes with no pupils or highlights, thick short curved eyebrows that carry all the emotion, small simple line mouth, no nose or just a tiny curve, small rounded hands with clear finger outlines. Characters have flat colors with only one soft shadow tone.
Backgrounds are MORE detailed and semi-realistic than the characters: real Indian everyday locations (old plastered walls with peeling paint, blue wooden doors, tube lights, steel utensils, tiled floors, electric wires, scooters), painted with soft muted colors, light film grain / paper noise texture over the whole image, gentle natural daylight, slightly desaturated warm palette (dusty blues, faded pinks, beige, grey). Mild depth of field: background characters are smaller and slightly softer. Mood: quiet, relatable, emotional, cinematic composition like a still from a short animated story.
```

## 2. CHARACTER BLOCKS (paste only the characters who appear)

```
BOY (main character): Indian young man, early 20s, slim. Big messy voluminous curly black hair with loose curls falling on the forehead. Peach skin, dot eyes, thick dark eyebrows. Wears a muted teal-green (#4E8A7E) full-sleeve cotton button-up shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbows and collar open, untucked; dark charcoal-black straight trousers; chunky off-white sneakers with grey soles; black round-dial analog wristwatch on his left wrist. Sometimes a dark grey crossbody/backpack bag.
```

```
GIRL (main character): Indian young woman, early 20s. Long thick dark-brown/black wavy hair with a centre-ish side parting, tied in one long loose braid falling over her shoulder, a few loose strands framing her face. Peach skin with pink blush, dot eyes, expressive thick eyebrows. Small round white pearl stud earring. Wears a deep maroon / wine-red (#8E2A3A) cotton kurta with small pink floral block-print motifs and a decorative print band on the sleeve cuffs, and a cream / off-white (#EDE0C8) cotton dupatta draped around her neck and over both shoulders.
```

```
TRAIN BOY (side character, for "thirst" scenes): Indian man, mid 20s, short spiky messy black hair (NOT curly), peach skin, dot eyes, thick eyebrows. Wears a dusty purple-blue (#5B5A9A) full-sleeve button shirt with sleeves rolled up, dark grey trousers, brown leather backpack.
```

```
DADI (grandmother, side character): Elderly Indian woman, around 70, kind face. Grey-silver hair combed back into a low bun, a few soft wrinkle lines near the eyes, gentle closed-mouth smile, dot eyes. Wears a maroon kurta with faint floral print and a heavy charcoal-grey woollen shawl wrapped over her shoulders.
```

## 3. NEGATIVE BLOCK (paste last, every time)

```
AVOID: any text, letters, captions, subtitles, logos, watermarks, signatures or speech bubbles; realistic or 3D human faces; anime big eyes; Pixar/3D render look; extra fingers or deformed hands; glossy plastic skin; neon or oversaturated colours; Western-looking architecture; borders or frames around the image.
```

## 4. SCENE PROMPTS (the 8 reference shots, in story order)

### Scene 1: Thirsty on the train
```
SCENE: Interior of a crowded Indian Railways sleeper/general coach in daytime. TRAIN BOY sits on a blue cushioned seat, slumped and exhausted, body angled toward the camera, head tilted slightly back. He grips a clear crinkled 1-litre plastic mineral-water bottle with a blue cap, the bottle mouth pressed to his lips, drinking desperately. Expression: eyebrows tilted up in relief/suffering, mouth open on the bottle, a single sweat drop on his temple. To the left, a large window with horizontal metal bars, motion-blurred green fields rushing past. Behind his seat on the right, an older passenger with curly hair and a moustache in a red shirt sleeps with his head back and eyes closed. Metal luggage rack overhead, a red bag on it. Brown-and-tan backpack on the seat beside him. Camera: medium shot, slightly high angle, character fills centre of frame.
```

### Scene 2: Sipping slowly
```
SCENE: Same Indian train coach, daytime, TRAIN BOY now sits at the small grey metal window-side table. He holds a nearly full clear plastic water bottle (blue cap) upright on the table with his right hand, and with his left hand points his index finger at the water level while his thumb is under his chin, as if measuring how much to drink. Expression: one eyebrow raised and furrowed, small frown, cautious and calculating. Three small black motion lines near his pointing finger. A paper train ticket lies on the table in front of him. Window with metal bars on the right showing green fields and blue sky, overhead luggage rack with a red bag, ceiling fan and tube lights on the left, two passengers seen from behind in the aisle on the left. Brown leather backpack at the bottom-left edge. Camera: medium close-up, eye level.
```

### Scene 3: Dadi's glass of water
```
SCENE: Courtyard of an old rural/small-town Indian house in soft afternoon light. DADI stands in the centre-left, upper body visible, holding a small clear glass of water with both hands close to her chest, looking at it with a calm contented smile. Behind her: beige plastered wall with a decorative cement jaali (lattice) vent, a blue-grey wooden door, a woven charpai cot at far left. On the right, a large shiny steel water matka/dispenser with a tap sits on a metal stand, with a smaller steel pot below it. A red-and-white checked gamchha towel hangs on a rod at top-right, green leaves peeking in at the top corner. Camera: medium shot, eye level, warm peaceful mood.
```

### Scene 4: She calls, he picks up instantly
```
SCENE: Narrow old Indian street/lane, daytime. Foreground left: GIRL seen from behind her right shoulder (over-the-shoulder shot), only the side of her face visible with a soft happy smile, her braid and cream dupatta in view. She holds a modern smartphone with a dark purple case in both hands; the screen shows an incoming call: circular avatar of the BOY at the top, sound-wave arcs on both sides, and a big green round call button her thumb is pressing, with small black "ringing" motion marks beside it. Background right: at the end of the lane, the BOY stands small in the distance, smiling, hands in pockets, backpack on. Lane has peeling plastered walls, a blue wooden double door, a rolled-down metal shutter, a stone bench, overhead electric wires, a parked scooter at the far right edge, faded buildings in the haze. Camera: over-the-shoulder, strong depth, phone is sharp and large in the frame.
```

### Scene 5: He comes running
```
SCENE: Busy old Indian street, bright day. BOY runs toward the camera in a big energetic stride, left foot forward, dust puffs kicking up behind his sneakers, speed lines around his legs. He holds his phone in his right hand at chest height looking at it and smiling widely with an open happy mouth, left arm swinging back showing the wristwatch, dark crossbody bag bouncing on his hip. Three small black excitement marks near his head. Background: weathered pink and cream two-storey buildings with a balcony railing and blue wooden doors and window grills, tangled electric wires overhead, a cycle-rickshaw and a man walking in the distance on the left, a silver scooter parked on the right, a manhole cover on the road at bottom-left. Camera: full body, low-ish angle, dynamic motion, character centred.
```

### Scene 6: She's bored, he's relaxed
```
SCENE: Split composition, daytime. Left half foreground: GIRL in close-up profile facing right, resting her cheek on her fist, elbow on a wooden table, bored and annoyed: one eyebrow lowered, eyes half-lidded looking sideways, small frown. Right half, seen through a doorway with a blue painted wooden frame: a casual college canteen / café with big grid windows and a hanging pendant lamp. BOY sits leaning far back on a wooden chair, both hands behind his head, eyes closed, peaceful smile, feet up on a small round table that has an open laptop and an open notebook; his backpack hangs on the chair. Two blurred students sit at tables in the background. Camera: close-up foreground + medium background, strong depth contrast.
```

### Scene 7: "Kuch nahi hai yaar" (she brushes him off)
```
SCENE: Middle-class Indian living room, daytime. Left foreground: GIRL sits sideways on a sofa with a brown-and-cream diamond-pattern upholstery, her left arm draped over the armrest, right hand raised palm-out in a dismissive "leave it" wave, with three small black motion arcs next to her hand. Expression: one eyebrow raised, the other furrowed, eyes half-lidded, mouth open mid-sentence, annoyed and dismissive. Right background: BOY sits alone on a matching patterned armchair, leaning forward with elbows on his knees, hands clasped, sad worried eyebrows, looking down. Behind: lavender-grey wall, a wooden window with a floral curtain, a framed old family photo on the wall above him, a wooden side table with a glass at the bottom-right edge. Camera: medium shot, girl large in foreground, boy smaller and isolated in background.
```

### Scene 8: Water is taken for granted
```
SCENE: Indian home kitchen, daytime. Left foreground: a stainless-steel tap dripping one big water drop, blue-grey wall tiles behind it, a white wall-mounted water purifier with a blue panel and pipes above, steel tumblers on a shelf. Centre foreground, very large and detailed: a clear glass tumbler half-filled with water standing on a wet grey granite counter, condensation droplets on the glass, small puddles and splashes around its base. Behind the glass: GIRL sits at the counter, chin resting on her hand, looking sideways at the glass with a bored, unimpressed expression: one eyebrow raised, small frown. Far right background, through a doorway: BOY sits alone on a bed's edge in another room, slumped with hands on knees, sad and ignored. A steel sink is built into the counter. Camera: low angle at counter level, glass dominant, characters behind it.
```

## 5. TEMPLATE FOR ANY NEW SCRIPT LINE

Fill in every bracket. Leaving parts vague is what makes generations drift.

```
SCENE: [Location: specific Indian place + time of day]. [Main character name] [exact pose: what each hand, arm and leg is doing] [holding/using exact object]. Expression: [eyebrows], [eyes], [mouth], [extra marks: sweat drop / motion lines / excitement marks]. [Second character: where in frame, pose, expression]. Background: [4–6 concrete Indian props: walls, doors, utensils, vehicles, wires]. Camera: [close-up / medium / full body / over-the-shoulder], [eye level / low / high angle], [what is foreground vs background].
```

## 6. TIPS FOR MATCHING THE LOOK EXACTLY

1. **Use reference images.** In ChatGPT or Gemini, upload one of your favourite generated frames and say: *"Use this exact art style and these exact characters"* before the prompt. This does more for consistency than any wording.
2. **Make one character sheet first.** Generate the BOY and GIRL standing side by side on a plain background (front view) using the style + character blocks, then attach that image to every later prompt.
3. **Same chat thread.** Keep generating all scenes in one conversation so the tool remembers the look.
4. **Square frames.** Ask for 1:1 every time; add your hook text and captions later in an editor (CapCut, InShot).
5. **If faces come out too realistic**, add: *"faces must be very simple: dot eyes, no nose, flat skin, like a minimalist webcomic."*
6. **If it adds text**, repeat at the end: *"Absolutely no text anywhere in the image."*
