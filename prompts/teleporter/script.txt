To determine whether that person is actually you, philosopher Derek Parfit proposed a variation in 1984. The machine makes the copy, but this time, it doesn't destroy you. You remain on Earth and watch on a screen as someone with your face starts talking on Mars. You are still here, so that person is someone else. Yet they are the exact same person who arrives when the machine does destroy you.
Parfit concluded that what matters are your memories and your character, not remaining the exact same person. Other philosophers consider it simply a way to die. And we could never test it, because the only person capable of answering is the one who arrives—and they will always say it worked.
