If someone finds reasons to touch you during normal conversation, this is what it means. Most people either overthink it into a full romantic conclusion or dismiss it completely as nothing. Both readings miss what psychology actually says about what's happening, because casual touches can mean many different things. Instead, use this simple psychological move to know what it means. Intentional touch during conversation is almost never accidental. Touch is one of the most primal signals of comfort and connection. And when someone initiates it without a clear social reason, their nervous system is telling you something their mouth hasn't said yet. So, first, do nothing dramatic. Just notice it. Stay relaxed. Don't pull away, but don't chase it either. Hold eye contact for one second longer than normal after the touch. Let a small, natural smile form and continue the conversation, because that one response tells them their signal was received without you saying a word.
