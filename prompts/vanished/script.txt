People who may have changed the world, but vanished.
Number one: Nikola Tesla promised the globe free wireless electricity. The moment investors realized they couldn't meter it, they killed his funding. Upon his death, federal agents seized his research. It never saw the light of day.
Number two: Stanley Meyer engineered a vehicle that ran entirely on water. He proved it worked in public. Weeks later, he collapsed at a restaurant screaming he was poisoned. His prototype vanished overnight.
Number three: MIT scientist Eugene Mallove chased cold fusion, a doorway to infinite clean energy. Before he could expose his findings, he was brutally murdered. The science faded to black.
Number four: Royal Rife invented a frequency machine that targeted and shattered cancer cells without harming healthy tissue. Instead of a Nobel Prize, his lab was raided, his equipment ruined, and his name erased.
