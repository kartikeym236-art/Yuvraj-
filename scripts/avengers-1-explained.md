# Avengers 1 Explained — 90-Second Script

**Runtime:** ~90 sec (~235 words) · **Format:** Vertical short (Reels / Shorts / TikTok)

---

**[0:00–0:05] HOOK**
*(Fast zoom on Loki smirking in the glass cage)*
Loki **wanted** to get caught. And that one move almost destroyed Earth.

**[0:05–0:15] THE SETUP**
*(Cosmic shot → Tesseract glowing)*
A mysterious alien named The Other (working for someone way scarier) gives Loki a deal: an army called the Chitauri, in exchange for one thing. The Tesseract.

**[0:15–0:25] THE HEIST**
*(Loki teleports into the SHIELD base)*
Loki shows up, steals the cube, and with a glowing scepter, takes over Hawkeye's mind in **two seconds**. Nick Fury's facility collapses behind him.

**[0:25–0:38] THE ASSEMBLY**
*(Quick cuts: Tony, Cap, Banner, Natasha, Thor)*
So Fury does the craziest thing possible. He brings together a billionaire with an ego problem, a 90-year-old soldier, a man who turns into a monster when he gets angry, a spy, and a literal god.
*(Beat)* What could go wrong?

**[0:38–0:52] THE TWIST**
*(Helicarrier, team arguing, scepter glowing)*
Everything. Loki surrenders on purpose. Locked on their own ship, his scepter quietly turns the heroes against each other. Then Hawkeye attacks, Banner hulks out, and Loki kills Agent Coulson.

**[0:52–1:00] THE TURN**
*(Fury tossing bloody Captain America cards)*
Fury uses that death to give them a reason. That's the moment they stop being strangers and become **a team**.

**[1:00–1:15] THE BATTLE**
*(360° team shot in New York)*
A portal rips open above Stark Tower. Aliens flood New York. Cap calls the shots, Hawkeye's on the rooftops, and Hulk? Hulk turns Loki into a rag doll. *"Puny god."*

**[1:15–1:25] THE SACRIFICE**
*(Nuke streaking toward Manhattan)*
Then the government launches a **nuke** at the city. Tony grabs it, flies it **through the portal** and sees an alien fleet in deep space… before his suit goes dark.

**[1:25–1:30] THE PAYOFF**
*(Mid-credits: Thanos turns and smiles)*
Hulk catches him. They win. But in deep space, someone turns around… and smiles.
**That's where Infinity War really began.**
